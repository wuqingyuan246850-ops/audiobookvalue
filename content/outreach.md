# AudiobookValue Outreach Kit

Use these ready-made snippets to build backlinks and drive traffic. Replace [SLUG] and [BOOK] with the actual page.

## Reddit (r/audiobooks, r/Fantasy, r/litrpg)

Post title idea: "Built a free tool that ranks Audible audiobooks by rating, length, and value"

Body:
> I got tired of wasting Audible credits on short or disappointing listens, so I built a free list that ranks audiobooks by rating, runtime, and value per credit. It updates daily and every book links to the Audible free trial. Example: [Best Fantasy Audiobooks](https://audiobookvalue.com/best-fantasy-audiobooks) and [Best Audiobooks Over 20 Hours](https://audiobookvalue.com/best-audiobooks-over-20-hours). Hope it saves someone a credit or two.

Tip: only post in communities where self-promotion is allowed; answer questions first and mention the site only when relevant.
