Overview

The Deal is the fan-favorite first Off-Campus novel: a hockey star and a music student fake a relationship to get what they both want.

What is this audiobook?

The Deal is the first book in Elle Kennedy's Off-Campus series, pairing hockey captain Garrett with tutor Hannah in a fake-dating arrangement. Christian Fox narrates this 9-hour romance.

Who is it for?

For sports-romance and fake-dating fans who want banter, steam, and real emotional depth. It solves the problem of finding a romance series that stays fun without getting shallow.

When should you listen?

Great for commutes and evenings; the college setting and fast pacing make it easy to binge. A relaxed, entertaining listen.

What makes it different?

Garrett and Hannah feel genuinely real, with chemistry that lands in both text and narration. The Deal is widely credited with defining the modern hockey-romance boom.

Absolutely. Start your free trial, and the fake-dating setup hooks you in the first chapters. It is a comfort read you will want to revisit.
