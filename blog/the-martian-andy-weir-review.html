Overview

The Martian is the survival classic that launched Andy Weir's career: Mark Watney fights to stay alive on Mars with duct tape, potatoes, and relentless optimism.

What is this audiobook?

The Martian by Andy Weir follows astronaut Mark Watney as he survives alone on Mars after being presumed dead. Wil Wheaton narrates Watney's logbook-style adventure over nearly 11 hours.

Who is it for?

For science lovers and fans of survival stories who want humor with their tension. It solves the problem of finding a smart page-turner that works perfectly in audio.

When should you listen?

Great for commutes or lunchtime listening; the logbook chapters are short and punchy. The problem-solving structure makes it easy to pause and resume.

What makes it different?

The book inspired the hit movie, but the audiobook adds Watney's inner voice and humor. Wil Wheaton's energetic narration makes the science feel like part of an adventure.

Yes. Start your free trial, and Watney's first log entry sets the tone. It is a fast, satisfying listen for any sci-fi fan.
