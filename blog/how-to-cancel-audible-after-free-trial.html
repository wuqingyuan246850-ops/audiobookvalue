How to Cancel Audible After the Free Trial

Blog Index

You can cancel Audible during the trial and still keep any audiobooks claimed with your free credit.

  1. Step 1: Sign in at audible.com and open Account Details.
  2. Step 2: Under Membership, choose Cancel Membership.
  3. Step 3: Confirm the cancellation; you keep trial credits and purchases.
  4. Step 4: Your membership ends at the current billing cycle with no further charge.
  5. Step 5: If you change your mind later, you can resubscribe and rejoin the trial terms available at that time.
